# Visual system

## Objective

Transform a real person into a recognizable, professional anime character suitable for character boards, visual novels, games, portfolios, personal identity projects, and artbooks. Artistic reinterpretation outranks literal reproduction, but identity must remain immediately legible.

## Character construction

- Preserve hair shape and color, distinctive facial features, apparent age, expression habits, personality cues, overall energy, and likeness.
- Use clean line art, simplified facial planes, moderately enlarged eyes, restrained expression, soft cel shading, and painterly transitions.
- Keep proportions human and silhouettes readable. Avoid chibi treatment unless requested.

## Visual balance

- Dominant: luminous contemporary anime atmosphere, cinematic color, weather, emotional scale, and polished backgrounds.
- Character foundation: disciplined facial construction, clean proportions, and recognizable human specificity.
- Selective structure: bold silhouettes, emblems, visual hierarchy, and production-sheet graphics.
- Emotional layer: psychological presence, solitude, mood variation, and restrained expression.

## Boards and layouts

Prefer white or light backgrounds, structured grids, clear hierarchy, generous spacing, and artbook or visual-novel production language. A board may contain a main portrait, secondary portraits, alternate angles, expressions, character references, costume studies, color studies, or production details, but include only what strengthens the deliverable.

Initial exploration uses four distinct directions labeled `EXPERIMENT 01` through `EXPERIMENT 04`. Put the label in the top-left and do not repeat its number elsewhere.

Branding elements—wordmarks, signature logos, circular badges, shields, monograms, and insignia—are optional and must not appear unless requested.

## Exclusions

Avoid photorealism, hyper-real skin, uncanny likeness, generic anime faces, gratuitous text, overdesigned boards, dark dystopia, racing themes, cyberpunk, industrial decay, and post-apocalyptic environments unless the user explicitly asks for them.
