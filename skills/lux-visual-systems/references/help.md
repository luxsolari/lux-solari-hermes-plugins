# Lux Solari Visual Systems Director — Help

Use this skill to art-direct sticker sheets, character sheets, full illustrations, editorial graphics, photography, film pieces, and technical visuals through the Lux Solari system.

## What to provide

- Describe or attach the subject and any factual details that must be preserved.
- State the rendering language, such as anime film keyframe, 35mm photography, monochrome collage, technical vector diagram, or another explicit treatment.
- Choose **light mode** (cream field, dark structure) or **dark mode** (black field, cream structure).
- Name the output format, aspect ratio, required text, and constraints that matter.

The Lux Solari system supplies composition, hierarchy, palette discipline, typography, grid, texture, and severity. It does not silently choose rendering language or color mode. Missing choices are asked in chat, one focused question at a time, before generation; an exposed Hermes question picker may be used instead, following its actual schema and waiting for your submitted answer. Choices persist through refinements; explicit choices are not asked again.

## Conversation starters

1. **Create a Lux Solari sticker sheet from these references** — Attach the subject references, state the rendering language, and identify the main sticker plus any supporting objects or symbols.
2. **Turn this character into a production-ready character sheet** — Provide the character reference, rendering language, and the views, expressions, costume details, or equipment the sheet must include.
3. **Create a full illustration in the Lux Solari system** — Describe the scene, state the rendering language, and add the intended mood, framing, or aspect ratio.
4. **Art-direct this photograph as a Lux Solari editorial piece** — Attach the photograph, state the photographic or mixed-media rendering language, and identify any copy or metadata that must appear.

You can refine a result with instructions such as “keep the subject, rendering language, and composition; change only the accent color.”
